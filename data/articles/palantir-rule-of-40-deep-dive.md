  - "AIP is expanding addressable market. Moving from data integration (the legacy Ontology platform) to AI-powered inference against that data stack opens enterprise buyers who previously found PLTR too complex."
  - "57% EBITDA margin means the company is already highly profitable at scale — not burning cash to fund growth."

:::bullets

  - "U.S. commercial deceleration is a live risk. 40% U.S. commercial growth sounds exceptional until you remember that PLTR's commercial segment started from a small base. The absolute dollar magnitude of incremental commercial revenue needs to keep accelerating for the multiple to hold."
  - "Government concentration risk is underpriced. PLTR's most durable contracts also represent its most politically exposed segment. A defense budget renegotiation or a contract restructuring would hit the narrative faster than the fundamentals."
  - "Valuation demands perfection. 287x forward P/E gives the stock essentially zero room for a growth scare, a government shutdown, or a commercial beat that isn't large enough."
:::

:::paragraph
text: The Rule of 40 framework — revenue growth rate plus EBITDA margin — is a quality-at-scale screen. A score above 40% is considered healthy for a mature software business. PLTR's 127% puts it in rare company: the kind of number that makes fundamental investors pay attention before they even check the multiple.
:::

:::paragraph
text: Drilling in: 70% total revenue growth was driven by 55% commercial revenue growth, with U.S. commercial specifically up 40%. The government segment — which PLTR has been trying to de-emphasize narratively — still anchors the base and provides the contract durability that makes the commercial expansion credible to enterprise buyers.
:::

:::paragraph
text: The 119% NRR tells the upsell story: existing customers are spending more year-over-year. That is exactly what a platform flywheel should produce. AIP, PLTR's AI-enabled data operating system, is the mechanism through which existing government contracts are expanding and new commercial logos are being added.
:::

:::quote
text: "A 127% Rule of 40 score is one of the best in software. The question is what multiple that score deserves when the market has already assigned it."
:::

:::verdict
label: Bottom line
text: "PLTR is an exceptional business executing well. The quality metrics — margin, retention, growth — are real. But at ~287x forward P/E, the equity is not priced for a good business; it is priced for a generational software franchise delivering at the high end of its range for years. The risk/reward at current prices favors keeping this on the watchlist rather than building a position. If the stock pulls back 25-30% on a growth scare, the calculus changes materially."
:::
