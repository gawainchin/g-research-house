OVERNIGHT MAP

Broad risk-off tone overnight. S&P 500 slipped 0.24% and Nasdaq fell 0.31%, but the tell is VIX — it spiked 7.95% to 18.87, the most elevated it's been in recent weeks. That means this isn't a clean drift; algo and macro funds are reaching for hedges. Hang Seng dropped 0.89% to 26,160, consistent with the cautious global mood rather than any China-specific catalyst firing. Semis led the damage on the US side: Intel warned and guided lower, Micron followed, and even some quality AI names sold off with the tape. Defensive names (GLD, XLU) were down too — genuine broad risk-off, not a rotation.

1. Intel guided below consensus on AI PC and data center demand — not a catastrophic miss, but the recovery narrative the market was pricing is now in doubt. Semis broadly caught after hours. This is the clearest fundamental event of the overnight.

2. VIX surged to 18.87 (+7.95%) — the highest in recent weeks. Not tied to any single headline; more of a macro fund positioning adjustment heading into month-end. Suggests market is starting to price in more uncertainty, not less.

ACTION BOARD

- Opportunity: NET bounced hard off the insider-selling lows. The thesis hasn't broken — insider plans executing on schedule don't change the cloud demand backdrop. Keep in the monitor bucket, don't add blindly, but don't exit either.

- Risk: INTC -4.09% and MU -1.46% together signal the semiconductor recovery is not as clean as the narrative suggested. If you're carrying NVDA as core AI exposure, this is a warning sign worth watching. The semis complex may be pricing in peak capex anxiety before MU reports.

- Research: VIX at 18.87 with defensives down alongside risk assets is the important signal — this is not a rotation trade, it's broad de-risking. Is the macro finally catching up to equity valuations, or is this a temporary month-end positioning effect? That distinction drives everything for next week's positioning.
