Copper and commodity signals should be watched for follow-through.

ACTION BOARD

• OPPORTUNITY — NET bounced +1.4% off last week's insider-selling lows. The move is sentiment-driven, not fundamental — but that means the fundamental thesis is still intact. Worth a dedicated re-read of the position thesis before adding or cutting. Do not add blindly just because it bounced.

• RISK — AAPL is the portfolio's biggest loser at -2.52% and BABA/GDS all in red. The tariff escalation hitting China tech ADRs is a concrete, named catalyst — not generic macro fear. If tariffs expand in scope or duration, these positions need sizing review against the scenario matrix guidelines. The HK account's HK$135k idle cash is dry powder but should not be deployed into a spreading China tech selloff.

• RESEARCH — Gold's 2.83% drop is the outlier signal worth investigating. Gold typically rallies when markets price systemic risk; the fact it sold off alongside a US market retreat suggests either (a) margin calls forcing liquidations across assets, or (b) confidence returning enough to rotate out of hard assets. Which one it is changes the read on overall market health significantly. Worth cross-checking with copper and TLT for confirmation before treating it as a simple risk-off reversal.
